What We Do

You bring the community.
We bring the infrastructure.

Starting a youth club is hard. Safeguarding, compliance, planning, funding, communication — the admin alone stops most communities before they begin. Youth Corner takes that weight off, so a single passionate person or small organisation can stand up a real youth provision.

01

Operational infrastructure

Safeguarding documentation, compliance, session planning, reporting, and communications — handled through one AI-driven digital system built for lone and small-team youth workers.

02

Ready-made provisions

Choose from proven, ready-to-run programmes matched to your space and organisation — from Art Corner to a Youth Café — so you're never starting from a blank page.

03

A family, not a franchise

Join the Youth Corner family and share the same values, model, and support. One organisation, many locations.

Our Story

Why Youth Corner exists

Bryanna Bone
Founder, Youth Corner CIC
Founder & CEO, OXIE (Oxford Innovative Education CIC)
Author, The Dawn of AI

Bryanna founded and leads OXIE, the accelerator behind Youth Corner, and co-architects the digital infrastructure that powers it.

As a youth worker of 15 years and a budding edtech founder — with access to the innovative infrastructure that's accelerating tech start-ups, a sector that drew £9 billion of investment in the UK in 2024 alone — she saw the gap in the youth sector and knew exactly how to fill it.

"When we champion youth workers, communities thrive."

"Running even a small youth club in a rural village is equivalent to being an entrepreneur — finances, marketing, event planning, staff and volunteer management — and on top of all that, there is also safeguarding, fundraising, and pastoral care for young people.

In two years, I grew a tiny youth club to over 200 young people, four programmes a week, and several community projects. I learned exactly what it takes to make a youth provision thrive — and exactly what pain points are threatening them.

My story isn't unique. After speaking with youth workers across the UK, I realised the lack of practical infrastructure for small youth clubs is nation-wide. So I decided to build it myself: no more paper sign-in sheets, no more last-minute scrambles for volunteers, no more missed grant deadlines.

When we champion youth workers, communities thrive. Youth Corner is how we build a youth sector that's sustainable, supported, and here to stay."

— Bryanna Bone, Founder
