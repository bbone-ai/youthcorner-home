What We Do

No Pressure. Just Walk In.

01

Afterschool drop-in

An open-door space for young people to go after school. Nothing to join and nothing to prove before you're welcome.

02

Qualified youth workers

Every session is supervised by qualified youth workers, so young people and their families know they are in safe hands.

03

Food, drink and programmes

Food and drink are provided, alongside creative and nature recovery programmes that enrich young people and the wider community.

What Happens at Youth Corner

Creative and nature recovery programmes

Alongside the everyday open-door space, each Youth Corner runs a light menu of drop-in and community-led sessions, and acts as the gateway into OXIE's wider innovation and leadership opportunities.

Our Mission

A Family of Youth Clubs,
Rooted Locally

To open and run free, low-barrier youth clubs in towns across the UK — proven first at Charlbury, now scaling across West Oxfordshire — where young people are welcome exactly as they are, and where OXIE's wider innovation and leadership pathways, including youth-led ventures, become the natural next step for those who want it.

Our Team

Our Leadership Team

Bryanna Bone
Leadership Team, Youth Corner
Founder & CEO, OXIE (Oxford Innovative Education CIC)
Author, Heirs of AI

Bryanna founded and leads OXIE, the accelerator behind Youth Corner.

A qualified youth worker with 16 years’ experience, Bryanna has proven success in scaling eleven non-profit youth outreach initiatives across six cities in Canada and the UK.

"When we champion youth workers, communities thrive."

Dianne Augustine
Leadership Team, Youth Corner
Founder, Witney Youth Café

Dianne is the founder of the Witney Youth Café, created in response to the lack of safe and accessible third spaces for young people outside home and school. She provides pastoral care, mentoring and wellbeing support to young people across West Oxfordshire.

With over 20 years' experience in youth work, safeguarding and SEND support, Dianne helped launch the West Oxfordshire Youth Voice Oversight Committee. She previously founded and led The Daniel Project, a youth and community charity in Barking & Dagenham, supporting youth and their families escape gang violence.
